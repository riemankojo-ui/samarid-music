Frequently Asked Questions

Does it cost money to join?

No. Applying and creating a profile on FlexTune is 100% free. We only take a small percentage of royalties once you start earning.

Can I upload music I bought online?

No. You must own the rights to the music you upload. Uploading beats or songs you do not own will result in account termination.

How long does it take to go live?

Once you schedule a release in the Studio, it takes about 2-7 days for the major platforms (Spotify, Apple) to process it. FlexTune goes live instantly.
