Profile Guidelines

Artist Name: This is the main name your fans will see on all your songs, albums, and your FlexTune profile page. Make sure it matches how you are known publicly.

Second Name: If you have a last name or a secondary brand name, you can add it here. It helps with search engine optimization (SEO) so fans can find you faster.

Email Address: This email is very important. It is used for account security, password resets, and receiving important updates about your music releases.

Password: Only enter a new password if you want to change it. If you leave it blank, your current password will stay the same.

Account Security

Your data is safe with us. Never share your password with anyone. If you ever suspect someone else has access to your account, change your password immediately here.
